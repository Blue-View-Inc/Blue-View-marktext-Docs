# Blue View design sources

Copied from https://github.com/Blue-View-Inc/blueview-design at `a783be6fa9bccde7b3845e5a94a963f4cd116006` (v0.1.1).
The files are unmodified. Preserve LICENSES.md. These are Blue View-owned assets,
separate from the upstream MarkText MIT license.

TrueDocs marks were supplied from Neblina, `blueview-hub/web/static/brand/truedocs/`.

The app's `blueview-dark.theme.css` overrides the generic dark tokens with the
approved Deep Botanical palette from `blueview-hub/web/static/hub.css` on Neblina:
ground #141A16, cards #1C241E, borders #31402F, ink #E6E8DF, muted #9FAB99,
and moss accent #9FC57E. The vendor files here remain unmodified.
